# Geometric and Thermal Characterization of the Residential Building Stock of Álava

## 1. Introduction

This repository presents a methodology for the geometric and thermal characterization of the residential building stock of Álava (Spain).

The methodology combines Geographic Information Systems (GIS), cadastral information, digital surface models, climatic data and predictive models developed in Python to estimate the geometric characteristics and annual heating energy demand of residential buildings.

The workflow is divided into two main stages:

- **Geometric characterization:** processing cadastral and geospatial data using QGIS to obtain relevant building parameters.
- **Thermal characterization:** applying predictive models and climatic data in Python to estimate building thermal characteristics and annual heating energy demand.

The methodology is based on previous research conducted in Bizkaia and has been adapted to the specific cadastral characteristics of Álava.

## 2. Objectives

The main objectives are:

- To characterize the geometry of residential buildings using cadastral and geospatial information.
- To obtain relevant building parameters through GIS-based processing.
- To estimate the thermal characteristics of buildings using predictive models.
- To calculate the annual heating energy demand of residential buildings.
- To provide a reproducible methodology that can be applied to a wider geographical area.

## 3. Methodology

The methodology consists of two main stages:

1. **QGIS processing:** processing cadastral information and digital surface models to obtain the geometric parameters required for the analysis.
2. **Python modelling:** using predictive models and climatic data to estimate thermal characteristics and heating energy demand.

## 4. Repository structure

The repository is organized into folders containing the documentation, QGIS scripts, Python scripts, input data, examples and results.

## 5. Software requirements

The methodology uses QGIS and Python. The specific software versions and Python dependencies will be described in the documentation.

## 6. Outputs

The methodology generates building-level geometric and thermal parameters, including estimates of annual heating energy demand.

## 7. Limitations

The applicability and computational performance of the methodology depend on the availability and quality of the input data, as well as the size of the study area.
